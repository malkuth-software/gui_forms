LLVM 22.1.8 runtime licence notices (libc++, libc++abi, libunwind), the
files libcxx/LICENSE.TXT, libcxxabi/LICENSE.TXT and libunwind/LICENSE.TXT of
https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project/tree/llvmorg-22.1.8.
Linux programs link libc++ 22, libc++abi and libunwind statically
(cmake/llvm22.cmake; apt.llvm.org's libc++-22-dev, libc++abi-22-dev and
libunwind-22-dev on Ubuntu) and carry these notices; macOS and Windows take
theirs from their runtime trees (packaging/licenses.json).
