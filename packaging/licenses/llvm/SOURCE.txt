LLVM 22.1.8 runtime licence notices (libc++, libc++abi), the files
libcxx/LICENSE.TXT and libcxxabi/LICENSE.TXT of
https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project/tree/llvmorg-22.1.8.
Linux packages bundle the libc++ 22 and libc++abi shared libraries the build
linked (apt.llvm.org's libc++1-22 and libc++abi1-22 on Ubuntu) and carry these
notices; macOS and Windows take theirs from their runtime trees
(packaging/licenses.json).
