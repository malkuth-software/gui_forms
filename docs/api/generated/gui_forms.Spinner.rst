.. cpp:class:: Spinner final : public Control, public SpinnerCapabilities<Spinner>

   ``#include <gui_forms/controls/spinner.hpp>`` (:src:`source <include/gui_forms/controls/spinner.hpp>`)

   An indeterminate busy indicator: a ring of twelve dots, three of them lit, turning while :cpp:member:`active <gui_forms::Spinner::active>`.

   A spinner shows that work is under way, not how much; a :cpp:class:`ProgressRing <gui_forms::ProgressRing>` or a
   :cpp:class:`ProgressBar <gui_forms::ProgressBar>` shows an amount. An inactive spinner draws nothing. It takes
   no input and no focus.

   .. rubric:: Notes

   The ring turns once every 1.2 seconds, a frame per dot step, only while the
   spinner is active and drawn. With ambient motion suppressed (reduced motion,
   ambient motion turned off, or an occluded window) the lit dots stand still
   at twelve o'clock.

   To assistive technology an active spinner is a busy progress bar without a
   value, named by its accessible name; an inactive one is not exposed. A theme
   styles the dots as the progress family's ``track`` and, lit, its
   ``activity`` part in the selected state.
