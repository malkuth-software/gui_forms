gui_forms::Wizard
=================

.. cpp:namespace:: gui_forms

.. cpp:class:: Wizard final : public Control, public WizardCapabilities<Wizard>

   ``#include <gui_forms/controls/wizard.hpp>`` (:src:`source <include/gui_forms/controls/wizard.hpp>`)

   A wizard: a guided sequence of pages shown one at a time, with a header, an optional step indicator, and Back, Next (Finish on the last page) and Cancel buttons.

   The pages are the :cpp:class:`WizardPage <gui_forms::WizardPage>` children in child order; other children are
   ignored. The header shows the current page's title and description, and the
   step indicator names every page by its title. Next is available while the
   current page's ``can_advance`` is set, and Back on every page but the first.
   Finish raises :cpp:func:`finished <gui_forms::Wizard::finished>` and Cancel raises :cpp:func:`cancelled <gui_forms::Wizard::cancelled>`; the wizard itself
   stays as it is, and its owner closes it. Use a :cpp:class:`TabControl <gui_forms::TabControl>` when the user
   may visit the pages in any order.

   .. rubric:: Notes

   A press on a completed step in the indicator goes back to it, through
   :cpp:func:`page_changing <gui_forms::Wizard::page_changing>` like the buttons. Enter left unhandled by the focused
   control acts as Next or Finish, and Escape as Cancel.

   To assistive technology the wizard is a group named by the current page's
   title. A theme draws the header's ``caption`` (the title, in the heading
   face) and ``label`` (the description, secondary), and the ``divider`` above
   the buttons, all of the panel family; the indicator and the buttons are
   stock controls.
