gui_forms::PieChart
===================

.. cpp:namespace:: gui_forms

.. cpp:class:: PieChart final : public Control, public PieChartCapabilities<PieChart>

   ``#include <gui_forms/controls/pie_chart.hpp>`` (:src:`source <include/gui_forms/controls/pie_chart.hpp>`)

   A pie or doughnut chart: one slice per value, each a share of the total, with share labels and a legend.

   .. gf-preview:: controls/pie_chart
      :alt: A PieChart as the stock specimen shows it, in the windows-professional theme.

   A pie chart shows records described by :cpp:class:`PieSlice <gui_forms::PieSlice>`: a stable ID, a label, a
   value and an optional colour. Slices run clockwise from :cpp:member:`start_angle <gui_forms::PieChart::start_angle>` in the
   order given; a slice without a colour takes the theme's series colour for
   its position, as :cpp:class:`PlotView <gui_forms::PlotView>`'s series do, so the chart follows the theme. A
   doughnut is a pie with a hole: set :cpp:member:`doughnut_radius <gui_forms::PieChart::doughnut_radius>` to the hole's radius as
   a percentage of the pie's. It is the plots package's counterpart of Windows
   Forms' ``Chart`` with a ``Pie`` or ``Doughnut`` series.

   *Status:* provisional.

   .. rubric:: Notes

   To assistive technology a pie chart is a list whose items are the slices,
   each named by its label with its value and share as its value. Its parts
   are ``plot``, ``legend`` and ``focus``; each slice publishes ``slice``,
   ``slice_label``, ``legend_swatch`` and ``legend_label``, with the states
   hot, selected and focus_visible. A selected slice moves out from the centre.
