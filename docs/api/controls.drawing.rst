Drawing
=======

Owner-drawn, live and raster surfaces.

.. list-table::
   :class: autosummary longtable
   :widths: 35 65

   * - :cpp:class:`DrawingSurface <gui_forms::DrawingSurface>`
     - A control that draws whatever its paint callback draws.
   * - :cpp:class:`RasterCanvas <gui_forms::RasterCanvas>`
     - A zoomable, pannable view of a bitmap that the application owns and edits.
   * - :cpp:class:`NodeCanvas <gui_forms::NodeCanvas>`
     - An editor over a node graph: nodes with ports and lamps, joined by links, on an unbounded canvas that pans and zooms deeply.
   * - :cpp:class:`LiveSurface <gui_forms::LiveSurface>`
     - A thread-safe exchange that hands the newest frame from a pixel producer to the window.
   * - :cpp:class:`SubSurfaceHost <gui_forms::SubSurfaceHost>`
     - Region control that runs one threaded sub-surface with its own retained tree, sizes and reconfigures it, composes its LiveSurface frames into the window, and forwards input and keyboard focus to it.

      -- undo --------------------------------------------------------------------- undo reverts the newest applied step and redo reapplies the oldest reverted one; recording a new operation discards every reverted step. Each advances the revision once (never back to an older number, so caches see every change). An identity that undo made stale is live again after redo: undo and redo restore exact identities.    .. cpp:function:: GraphResult redo()

   .. cpp:function:: void begin_undo_group() noexcept

      Groups nest; the operations between the outermost begin and its end form one step (an empty group forms none). undo, redo, clear_undo and load are refused while a group is open.

   .. cpp:function:: GraphResult end_undo_group() noexcept

   .. cpp:function:: GraphResult rollback_undo_group() noexcept

      Cancels the open group: reverts every operation recorded since the outermost begin_undo_group, newest first and exactly as undo does (same slots, generations, free lists, IDs and bytes), removes the group's step so neither undo nor redo sees it, and closes every open level, inner ones included (a later end_undo_group returns no_group). Advances the revision once when it reverted anything. The redo steps that existed before the group were discarded by its first operation, as by any new operation, and stay discarded. Lamps are not recorded, so lamp writes inside the group stay. no_group when no group is open; nothing_to_undo when recording is off (maximum_steps 0): the group is closed and its operations stay. Never allocates.

   .. cpp:function:: GraphLoadResult load(std::string_view document)

      Replaces the graph's contents with the document's. The whole document is read and checked first (syntax, bounds, keys, links) into work storage bounded by the document; only then is the graph changed, with nothing left that can fail. The undo log is emptied: the loaded document is the start. One revision advance. May throw std::bad_alloc while building the work storage, before any change.

Validates first: throws std::invalid_argument for options outside their stated ranges (a non-finite or non-positive cell size, a cell cap outside 4 to maximum_spatial_cells, no cell per entry, too long a displaced list) or a present entry whose bounds are not valid_world_rect, and std::length_error for more than maximum_spatial_entries entries or a grid whose cell lists would not fit 32-bit offsets; the index is then unchanged. Storage is grown, never shrunk, so rebuilding a graph of the same size allocates nothing. If an allocation fails the index is left empty.

   .. cpp:function:: bool move_entry(std::uint32_t id, const WorldRect& bounds) noexcept

      Gives entry ``id`` new bounds. False, and nothing changes, when ``id`` is past the entries, the entry is absent, :cpp:func:`bounds <gui_forms::SpatialIndex::bounds>` is not valid_world_rect, or the entry would leave its cells while the displaced list is full (rebuild then). Never allocates.

   .. cpp:function:: bool remove_entry(std::uint32_t id) noexcept

      Makes entry ``id`` absent: no query matches it until a build. False when it is past the entries or already absent. Never allocates.

   .. cpp:function:: SpatialQueryResult query(const WorldRect& area, SpatialQueryMode mode, std::span<std::uint32_t> out) noexcept

      Writes into ``out`` the present entries matching ``area`` under ``mode``. An area that is not valid_world_rect matches nothing. Never allocates.

   .. cpp:function:: void clear() noexcept

      Empties the index (keeping its storage).

   .. cpp:function:: bool present(std::uint32_t id) const noexcept

      Whether entry ``id`` is present, and its current bounds (an empty rectangle at the origin for an absent or unknown entry).

.. cpp:class:: SubSurfaceThread final

   ``#include <gui_forms/surface_root/sub_surface/sub_surface.hpp>``

   The parent's side of one sub-surface: owns its thread (owner decision 9, the library's thread) and the channel the two sides share. Made, used and destroyed on the parent's thread. Nothing crosses but the configurations, the stop request and the published frames (house style 8).


   .. rubric:: Construction

   .. cpp:function:: SubSurfaceThread(std::unique_ptr<SubSurfaceContent> content, std::shared_ptr<LiveSurface> surface, std::shared_ptr<const Theme> theme, std::unique_ptr<PaintFramebuffer> text_measure, std::shared_ptr<const Theme> layered_user_theme = {})

      Starts the thread. It makes ApplicationResources of its own showing ``theme``, calls content.build_root(), makes a SubSurface of the root, and waits for the first configuration: it produces nothing before the first agreed size (resize rule 5). It publishes into ``surface``, reconfiguring it whenever the device size changes. ``text_measure``, if not null, is a framebuffer made by the parent whose painter measures the sub-surface's text for its whole life (any size; one pixel will do); without one, text is measured by the estimate. ``layered_user_theme``, if not null, is the parent's user theme under the layered policy (ApplicationResources::user_theme_ptr), selected in the sub-surface's resources before its root exists, so its rules reach every part there as they do in the parent (design note section 14); a user theme replacing the program's arrives as ``theme`` itself. Null content, surface or theme, or a ``layered_user_theme`` not compiled as a user theme, is std::invalid_argument; a thread the system refuses is std::system_error.

   .. cpp:function:: WindowDispatcher dispatcher() const

      Posts to the sub-surface's thread. Empty (posting throws, as an empty WindowDispatcher does) until the state is running; any thread may keep the handle after the sub-surface stopped, and posts then fail as posts to a closed window do.

   .. cpp:function:: SubSurfaceState state() const

   .. cpp:function:: std::string failure() const

      What the content threw, for state failed; empty otherwise.

   .. cpp:function:: SubSurfaceStatistics statistics() const

   .. cpp:function:: std::optional<Rect> text_input_caret() const

      The sub-surface's text input caret (SurfaceRoot::text_input_caret) in its own coordinates, as its thread last published it at the end of a turn; none while nothing there has focus (design note section 4: the input method follows it without a synchronous call).


   .. rubric:: Methods

   .. cpp:function:: void configure(SubSurfaceConfiguration configuration)

      Hands the sub-surface a new configuration and wakes it. Only the newest unapplied one is kept (configurations_coalesced counts the others). An epoch not above the last one sent is std::invalid_argument; after stop() the configuration is released and nothing changes.

   .. cpp:function:: std::uint64_t post_input(const SubSurfaceInput& input)

      Queues one input event for the sub-surface's tree and wakes it; the thread dispatches queued events in this order, after applying the newest configuration and before its next flush. Returns the event's sequence: 1 for the first, growing by one. A pointer move replaces a queued move of the same pointer at the back and takes the new sequence. After stop() or a failure, or while maximum_deferred_inputs events are queued, the event is discarded and 0 is returned.

   .. cpp:function:: void post_request_outcome(const SurfaceRequestOutcome& outcome)

      Queues the parent's answer to a request and wakes the sub-surface, which hands it to its tooltip route after its input, before its flush (an answer to request_tool_tip goes on to that request's answer target). Discarded after stop() or a failure.

   .. cpp:function:: std::shared_ptr<const SemanticSnapshot> take_semantics()

      The newest semantic snapshot the sub-surface published (design note section 5: an immutable snapshot, shared and never mutated across the threads; its nodes are in the sub-surface's own coordinates and runtime identities); null before the first. Taking it re-arms the notice: the next publication posts a new one. A snapshot walks the whole tree, so after the first the sub-surface builds the next only once this one is taken (or a listener is set): changes in between build nothing, and the snapshot taken then may be stale, in which case taking it has the newest built and published (with its notice).

   .. cpp:function:: void stop() noexcept

      Revokes the sub-surface (it publishes no frame from now on), asks its loop to stop, and joins the thread: the loop finishes its current flush, releases its tree on its own thread and exits (design note section 9). Idempotent.

   .. cpp:function:: void set_request_target(WindowDispatcher parent, std::weak_ptr<SubSurfaceRequestTarget> target)

   .. cpp:function:: void set_semantics_listener(WindowDispatcher parent, std::weak_ptr<SubSurfaceSemanticsListener> target)
