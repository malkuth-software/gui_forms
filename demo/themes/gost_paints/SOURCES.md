# GOST paint palette: sources and evidence

The colours of `gost-paints.theme.css` and the evidence for each. The sheet
and `theme.json` are generated by `tools/industrial_surfaces/gost_palette.py`
(`--check` verifies them and recomputes every class a colour). This is a
summary of the industrial-surfaces study's research, researched 2026-10-31:
`notes:experiments/industrial-surfaces/research/soviet_paints.md` on the
notes `gui_forms` branch, which keeps the full tables, the Russian names and
every quotation.

## How the colours are named

Soviet enamel standards (GOSTs) give each colour as a pair of swatches from
the colour card index, the «Картотека образцов (эталонов) цвета
лакокрасочных материалов» (the Kartoteka). The pair bounds the tolerance; it
is not two colours (ГОСТ 12.4.026-76, Appendix 2). The sheet names each
colour by the first card of its pair and by its evidence class:

- `--gost-a-<card>`, **class a**: a numeric value printed by a GOST. All of
  them are from ГОСТ 22133-86, Table 7: CIE x, y and reflectance, measured
  on the Радуга-2 instrument under illuminant C, specular component
  included.
- `--gost-b-<card>`, **class b**: a numeric value from a secondary source,
  here the laz-legend.ru card list (S13), whose method is unstated (probably
  scanned or sampled from a screen).
- **Class c**: a name or card number with no value found. Not in the sheet.

No value was eyeballed or sampled from a photograph.

## Conversion of the class a values

From ГОСТ 22133-86's x, y and reflectance R: XYZ with Y = R / 100; Bradford
adaptation from illuminant C (x .31006, y .31616; the CIE 1931 2° observer is
assumed, as the GOST does not state it) to D65 (x .31271, y .32902); the
IEC 61966-2-1 sRGB matrix and transfer curve; rounded to bytes. No value is
out of gamut. Two caveats:

- the GOST values include the specular component, so a glossy enamel looks
  slightly darker and more saturated than its value;
- each value is the first swatch of the pair, not the tolerance's centre.

ГОСТ 22133-86 Appendix 4, Table 11 recommends two-colour schemes of the same
hue at different lightness, at most three colours a machine besides signal
colours: pale mint 319 with grey-green 365 or teal 397; grey-green 365 with
ivory, pale mint, teal or white night; teal 397 with pale mint, grey-green or
white night; white night 858 with brown, grey-green, teal, grey, grey-silver
or dark grey. The documented "Soviet machine" looks are pale mint over
grey-green, and grey-green or white night over dark teal. Its Appendix 5
lists hammer enamels (МЛ-165, ГОСТ 12034-77) to go with them, set by control
samples only (class c).

## What is not documented

- No official conversion from the Kartoteka to RAL, Munsell or Lab exists
  for the general palette; ГОСТ Р 12.4.026-2001 co-lists only the signal
  colours.
- No value was found for the turquoise, sea-wave and grey-blue swatches
  beyond the class b list.
- No source names the paint of power-plant control rooms: "Chernobyl green"
  and the like are impressions from photographs.
- The GOST texts were read from web transcriptions with OCR damage; the
  НЦ-256 and МЛ-152 pairs were aligned by order and need checking against
  scans.
